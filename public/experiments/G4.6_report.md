# Project Genesis G4.6 — Integrated Module 2 Test

## Purpose
Integrate external world-model knowledge, out-of-sample prediction, and targeted intervention in one generated artificial world. Test whether these capabilities leave observable advantages under novel internal probes.

## Design
- 300 generated worlds with hidden nonlinear parameters.
- 16 unpredictable internal probes per world.
- Four paired conditions: true modeler, heuristic controller, random controller, no controller.
- First eight probes use calibration-range magnitudes; final eight use held-out magnitudes.
- All controller arms receive the same world and probe sequence.
- Controllers observe each realized probe before forecasting/intervening. They do not receive future probes.
- The true modeler receives the exact generating equations and parameters. The heuristic does not know coupling or nonlinear saturation.

## Interpretation
The true modeler has zero forecast error by construction because it uses the same equations and parameters that generate the world. On held-out probes it intervenes in 41.2% of episodes, and when it intervenes it targets the affected region correctly in 100.0% of those cases. Its mean one-step target-region gain is 1.92 relative to the paired no-controller counterfactual.

The heuristic controller intervenes in 19.6% of held-out probes and also produces a positive mean gain (0.93), while the random controller produces a much smaller mean gain (0.17). The true modeler's mean gain is higher, but its paired gain is positive in only 71.7% of worlds. This variability matters: average advantage does not mean universal superiority.

## Module status
- Module 1: Genesis-3 provides prior evidence that a small artificial life world can run and evolve; G4.6 adds a separate generated nonlinear resource world.
- Module 2: this is an integrated constructive demonstration of world-model knowledge, out-of-sample prediction, and targeted intervention in a toy world. It is not yet proof that an internal observer can infer a conscious external creator, nor a general proof of relative divinity.
- Module 3: not tested here. It requires a separate philosophical argument from constructive possibility to the possibility of our world's external origin.

## Limitations
Two state variables, simple resource transfer, one-step forecasts, fixed controller architectures, and no consciousness. The exact-model controller is given the true equations and parameters; autonomous discovery is not tested. The internal observer does not see private forecasts or model parameters. Intervention gain is measured over one step, not long-run welfare or system-level stability.
